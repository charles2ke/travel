Departure + arrival flow

Before Dublin, then JFK Terminal 4

Departure checklist

  • Aim to reach Dublin Airport 3.5–4 hours before a US flight
  • US passport control and immigration happen in Dublin before boarding
  • Keep passport with B1/B2 visa, first hotel address, and return flight details ready
  • Have boarding pass saved offline

Arrival plan

  • Delta lands at JFK Terminal 4 on 23 May at 3:49 PM ET
  • After landing: baggage claim, then taxi or AirTrain
  • Best transfer after a long flight: taxi or Uber to Midtown
  • Typical taxi cost to Manhattan: about $70–90 total

Phone setup for US

SIM usage plan

  • Lyca Ireland → main data connection in US
  • Vi India → OTP, banking SMS backup
  • Disable mobile data on Vi to avoid roaming charges
  • 5–10 GB data enough for maps, Uber, browsing

Phone settings

  • Mobile data → Lyca SIM
  • Turn OFF automatic data switching
  • Keep both SIMs active for calls/SMS
  • Enable roaming only on Lyca

Backup option

  • If Lyca roaming expensive → use eSIM
  • Good options: Airalo or Nomad
  • Activate before flight or at JFK

Before departure

  • Download offline maps: NYC, DC, Boston
  • Test Revolut / Google Pay
  • Carry power bank

  • 2 payment cards
  • $100–200 cash
  • Travel insurance details if you have them

  • 1 light sweater or hoodie
  • 1 light jacket
  • 1 jeans
  • 1 chinos or comfortable trousers
  • 7–9 underwear and socks
  • Comfortable walking shoes
Tech⌄

  • Charger cable

  • US plug adapter
